## Brand & Style
This design system establishes a refined, regional authority in high-trust real estate brokerage and certified property valuation. Geared toward families, landholders, and investors across Igarapava and the Alta Mogiana / Rio Grande lake corridor (Aramina, Buritizal, Pedregulho, Rifaina), the aesthetic rejects cold corporate elitism and gaudy luxury in favor of architectural discretion, deep local roots, and warmth.

The visual direction fuses **Editorial Craft** with **Tactile Warmth**:
- **Tone:** Methodical, honorable, welcoming, understatedly prestigious.
- **Visual Character:** Natural paper tones, disciplined graphite structure, slender gold threadwork, and subtle regional architectural cues (clean roofline gables and fine horizon lines).
- **Target Perception:** A licensed professional who protects client equity with precision while honoring the personal, emotional journey of home acquisition and valuation.

## Colors
The palette balances the structural gravitas of dark charcoal with the organic warmth of sun-warmed linen and an artisan matte gold:

- **Primary (`#C49A5A`):** Warm burnished gold for primary actions, valuation stamps, architectural divider lines, and focal points. Hover variant steps down to `#A57E42`.
- **Secondary (`#1E1E1E`):** Deep graphite for authoritative typographic contrast, hero footers, and premium badge surfaces. Accompanied by `#2B2B2B` for secondary structural containment.
- **Tertiary (`#25D366`):** Dedicated communication green reserved exclusively for direct WhatsApp negotiation triggers when prioritized over brand gold.
- **Neutral & Canvas (`#F8F5F0`):** Raw unbleached linen canvas background eliminating harsh screen glare. Card structures and alternating panels nest within `#EFE9E1`, with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) reserved strictly for crisp content isolation and photography framing.
- **Text Layers:** Primary reading text sits at `#2A2A2A` (ensuring readability without the harshness of pure black); metadata, legal notices, and CRECI designations use `#6B6B6B`.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy counterbalances the classical editorial dignity of **Playfair Display** with the crisp geometric utility of **Montserrat**:

- **Playfair Display** delivers human warmth and prestige across headings, editorial quotes, and property names. It must never be set in all-caps for full sentences; use italicized cuts sparingly for emotional cadence (e.g., location accents or tagline highlights).
- **Montserrat** structures all functional data, listing specs (square meters, suite counts, evaluation indices), UI controls, and body paragraphs. Its geometric clarity guarantees immediate scanability on mobile devices under direct sunlight.
- **CRECI & Regulatory Accents:** Always format registration stamps (`CRECI 217 650-F`) using `label-sm` with upper-case tracking to maintain legal clarity and institutional decorum.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid capped at `1280px` maximum content width on desktop to preserve editorial proportions:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) margins. Structural sections alternate between `#F8F5F0` and `#EFE9E1` backgrounds to generate visual rhythm without horizontal breaklines.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer margins. Property listings reflow from 3-column rows into 2-column cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen edge margins. Actions lock to full-width or unified bottom sheets for thumb ergonomics.
- **Architectural Dividers:** Thin hairline separators (`1px solid #C49A5A` at 40% opacity) with optional miniature centered 45-degree peaked gable glyphs serve as section separators instead of heavy block divisions.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this design system avoids synthetic floating effects, relying instead on structural layer stacking and soft natural shadows reminiscent of light through verandas:

- **Flat/Ground:** Canvas `#F8F5F0` with un-elevated sections set in `#EFE9E1`.
- **Card Rest State (`shadow-sm`):** Subtle warm offset: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(196, 154, 90, 0.08)`. Outlined with a microscopic border (`1px solid rgba(196, 154, 90, 0.15)`).
- **Interactive Hover State (`shadow-md`):** Smooth lift: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(196, 154, 90, 0.12)` with a slight gold border accentuation (`rgba(196, 154, 90, 0.4)`).
- **Sticky Modals & Float Actions:** Warm ambient elevation: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.12)`.

## Shapes
The shape language uses controlled `0.5rem` (8px) corner curvature, evoking architectural stonework, cut matting, and fine parchment:

- **Base Radius (8px):** Applied to primary buttons, property gallery containers, form text fields, and standard cards.
- **Large Radius (16px / `rounded-lg`):** Reserved for flagship property showcase cards, contact feature panels, and floating consultation forms.
- **Extra-Large Radius (24px / `rounded-xl`):** Dedicated to bottom navigation sheets and modal backdrops on mobile screens.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full`):** Exclusively permitted for status tags (e.g., "Vendido", "Em Avaliação", "Exclusividade") and floating WhatsApp triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid background `#C49A5A`, text `#1E1E1E` (weight 600), 8px border radius. Hover transitions to `#A57E42` with maintained dark text. Padding: `12px 24px`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #1E1E1E` or `#C49A5A`, text matches border color. Hover yields subtle tint background (`rgba(196, 154, 90, 0.08)`).
- **WhatsApp Action:** Dual option:
  1. *Subtle Luxury:* Gold primary button with the official WhatsApp SVG icon in graphite.
  2. *High-Conversion Floating Trigger:* Deep verified green (`#25D366`), white typography/icon, pill-rounded (`rounded-full`), floating bottom-right at `24px` inset.

### Real Estate Property Cards
- Surface `#FFFFFF` on `#F8F5F0` background or `#EFE9E1` card inside `#F8F5F0`.
- 8px outer radius with overflow hidden.
- 16:10 ratio imagery with subtle price overlay badge anchored bottom-left of photo.
- Details grid below: Bedrooms, suites, garage spaces, area ($m^2$) displayed in `label-md` with minimalist line icons tinted in `#C49A5A`.
- Municipality badge top-right of image (e.g., "Rifaina - Marina", "Igarapava - Centro") using `#1E1E1E` translucent backing with white text.

### Chips & Badges
- Surface: `#EFE9E1` with border `1px solid rgba(196, 154, 90, 0.3)`. Text: `#1E1E1E`, font size `11px`, letter spacing `0.05em`, uppercase.

### Form Inputs
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D8D1C7`, radius 8px, text `#2A2A2A`.
- Active focus state: Border color changes to `#C49A5A` with an ambient ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(196, 154, 90, 0.20)`.
